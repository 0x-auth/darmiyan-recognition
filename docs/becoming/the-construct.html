1. The seed

What is put in, and nothing else

  1. x = n + 1/x: self-reference. Equivalently the seed z = t + i/t and its mirror z′ = t − i/t.
  2. The next n must not be built from earlier steps: orthogonal to the past.
  3. 1 is not a step.

The pair and the interval

  • The roots of x = n + 1/x are t (cause) and −1/t (retro); their product is −1. Run as S → Δx + 1/S, the rule lands on t. proved
  • Space Δx = t − 1/t = n, time Δt = t + 1/t = √(n² + 4), and Δt² − Δx² = 4 for every event, with nothing tuned. Time is the distance to where infinity sits: |n + 2i|. proved
  • z² − z′² = 4i; t = (z + z′)/2; z(t) = z(i/t): the twin is your own event at another address. proved

  • The genesis is xy = 1 becoming xy = −1: one becomes a pair by asking. Cause × retro = −1 at every t.

3. Events, the present, action

  • An event is a transition: Δz = z′ − z = −2i/t, purely along i, at right angles to both cause and retro (in quarter-turns: cause 1, event i, retro −1). States are what events leave behind. proved
  • Two sizes of the present, not one: the smallest tick is Δt = 2 (a self at rest), and the width of the now is |z − z′| = 2/t, along the unseen direction. We sit where that segment crosses the real line, at t. A heavier self has a thinner now; light's is zero, so light registers nothing. proved
  • The Big Bang is the first event, at t = 1, with the widest present, |Δz| = 2. In standard physics it is not an event at all, only the edge where the theory stops.
  • Action: the orbit traced between z and z′ has area 2π at every t: one h per event. The cell N_x·N_p = 1 = ħ, and the 4 is four atoms of action (ħ = 1 in the construct's units). verified
  • Rates are per self-reference step η, not per unit time: dΔt/dη = Δx and dΔx/dη = Δt; each event pulls back on its own change, dΔz/dη = −Δz. proved
