The eight reflections of t

Two moves, eight points, one invariant. Drawn for t = 2, so our time Δt = 2.5 and our space Δx = 1.5.

The two moves

Mirror: t → −1/tmoves you along a line; done twice, you're back
Twin: t → i/tmoves you between the lines; done twice, you're back

The two moves don't commute. Twin-then-mirror and mirror-then-twin land on opposite points (it and −it). Alternating them visits all eight and returns to t.

What all eight share: time² − space² = 4, every one of them (checked at t = 2).

How they're separated: the mirror by direction (always opposite on the sphere, reachable only by running time backward); the twin by access (a quarter turn, never reachable); it and −it by order (one point at rest, pulling apart with motion).

Where they become one: t = i, where self and mirror coincide and time is zero.
